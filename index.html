Leo / Zeqing Yuan
Incoming Grad Student @MIT

I am joining MIT to pursue my Ph.D. this fall. Prior to that, I am completing my M.S. in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Srinivasa Narasimhan and Prof. Aswin Sankaranarayanan. I am broadly interested in computer vision, embodied agents and generative AI. My master's research focused on physics-based vision with light and heat, and my undergraduate work explored generative models and video understanding.

Previously, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science with the highest honor from Zhejiang University. During my undergraduate years, I had the privilege to work with Professors Yingcai Wu, Jake Zhao, Chunhua Shen and Jiajun Wu.
